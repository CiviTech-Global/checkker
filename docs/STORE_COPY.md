## Full Description (Google Play, ~2,000 chars)

**Checkker** is a fast-paced strategy game where chess tactics meet poker
scoring. Every turn you draw cards that decide which pieces you can move,
capture enemy pieces to build your score pile, and finish the game by
checkmate, timeout, or the strongest poker hand.

**Key features:**

- ♟️ **Card-driven chess** — Your hand controls the board. Wild Aces, royal
  pieces, and tactical traps keep every game fresh.
- ♠️ **Poker scoring layer** — Captured cards become your score pile; build
  pairs, straights, flushes, and full houses to win on points.
- ⚡ **Real-time multiplayer** — Ranked, casual, and private matches with
  built-in chess clocks.
- 🤖 **Adaptive AI** — Four difficulties and four personalities that learn from
  your play style.
- 🧩 **Daily puzzles + lessons** — Train with curated puzzles and coaching tips.
- 🏆 **Leaderboards + cosmetics** — Climb the ranks and unlock boards, pieces,
  and card backs.
- 🌐 **LAN play** — Face friends on the same local network, no internet needed.
- 🔒 **Free to play** — Optional testnet blockchain features; no real-money
  betting in v1.

Whether you are a chess purist, a poker strategist, or just love competitive
puzzle games, Checkker offers a deep, replayable duel in minutes.

Download now and make your opening gambit.
