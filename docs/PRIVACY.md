# Privacy Policy for Checkker

> **Template — review with legal counsel before publishing.**

Effective date: 2026-07-06

## 1. Introduction

Checkker (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a hybrid chess + poker strategy game. This
Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your
rights regarding that information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Wallet address (if you connect a crypto wallet).
- Username and optional avatar selection.
- Device identifier for guest accounts.

### 2.2 Gameplay Data
- Game moves, results, ratings, and match history.
- Bot configuration and puzzle progress.
- In-game chat messages (stored only for active matches and replay support).

### 2.3 Device & Usage Data
- Device type, operating system, and app version.
- Crash logs and basic analytics events (e.g., games started, features used).
- Push notification token (only if you opt in).

### 2.4 Blockchain Data
- Public on-chain transaction hashes when you deposit or withdraw funds.
- We do not store private keys or seed phrases.

## 3. How We Use Information

- Provide, operate, and improve the game.
- Match players, run leaderboards, and calculate ratings.
- Prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse.
- Send optional notifications (only with your consent).
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:

- Service providers hosting our servers and analytics.
- Law enforcement or regulators when required by law.
- Other players through public profiles and leaderboards (username, rating, and
  limited stats only).

## 5. Data Retention

- Game history and ratings are retained to support replays and leaderboards.
- Chat messages are deleted after the match ends or shortly thereafter.
- Account deletion requests will remove personally identifiable information
  within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for anti-fraud
  purposes.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent for optional features like notifications.

Contact us at: privacy@checkker.example

## 7. Children

Checkker is not intended for users under 13 (or the applicable age of digital
consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of material
changes through the app or by email.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@checkker.example.
